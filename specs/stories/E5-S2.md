# E5-S2 — Organizations & membership

**Epic:** E5
**Layer:** Service
**Group:** F
**Depends on:** E4-S1, E2-S2, E5-S1

## Description

Personal-org auto-creation, org lifecycle, and invitations. organization.type is personal or team; a personal account is an organization of one (D7) and all resource ownership is org_id.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. When `capabilities.personalAccounts`, signup auto-creates a personal org (owner = the user) in the same transaction; personal orgs expose no invites or members screens.
2. Self-service org creation makes the creator `owner`; at least one owner is always enforced and ownership transfer is supported.
3. Org deletion is owner-only, soft-deletes (`deleted_at`) with name confirmation and 30-day retention.
4. An invite (email, role, 7-day single-use token) can be sent, resent, revoked, and accepted; acceptance lands the invitee as a member with the assigned role.
