# E2-S2 — Background jobs & email delivery

**Epic:** E2
**Layer:** Service
**Group:** D
**Depends on:** E1-S4, E1-S2, E3-S3

## Description

platform-jobs wraps pg-boss (defineJob with Zod payloads, enqueue, cron schedule, workers in the API process); platform-email is a port with SMTP (nodemailer) and dev (Mailpit) adapters. All email is delivered through a queued job consuming branding and terminology; direct synchronous send is not exposed.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. `defineJob(name, zodSchema, handler)` rejects an enqueue whose payload fails the schema and accepts a valid one; the handler runs and the job completes.
2. A scheduled (cron) job registers and fires on its schedule.
3. `email.send({ to, template, data })` enqueues a delivery job; in dev the message appears in Mailpit and its subject/body reflect `branding.productName` and the active terminology.
4. Shipped jobs exist for email delivery, invite-expiry sweep, and soft-deleted-org purge.
