# E8-S4 — Export-workspace job & CSV email

**Epic:** E8
**Layer:** Service
**Group:** I
**Depends on:** E8-S2, E2-S2

## Description

"Export workspace" enqueues a pg-boss job that generates a CSV of the workspace’s tasks and emails it via the email port, exercising jobs + email together.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Triggering export enqueues a job whose handler produces a CSV containing the workspace’s tasks.
2. The CSV is delivered by email through the queue and appears in Mailpit in dev.
3. The job payload is Zod-validated and failures retry per the pg-boss config.
